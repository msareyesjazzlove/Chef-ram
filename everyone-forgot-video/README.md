# What If Everyone Forgot Who You Are? (3D animated, 1:30)

`everyone-forgot-who-you-are-3d.mp4` is a 1280×720, 30fps, 90-second 3D animated story with a soundtrack.

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Day 0: golden memory threads connect her to everyone, then snap |
| 0:10 | A stranger at home: her family doesn't recognize her and her photos go blank |
| 0:22 | Your work, unknown: her name fades from the restaurant sign |
| 0:34 | Alone in a crowd: rain, grey plaza, colors drain away |
| 0:46 | What is still yours? A light glows in her heart |
| 0:58 | So you start again: she cooks for strangers and new threads form |
| 1:12 | They feel it again: "Mama!" and the whole street lights up |

`source/` holds the Three.js scene (`forgot.js`), the frame renderer (`render.js`) and the soundtrack generator (`audio.py`).
