# What If You Made Your Mom's Dream Come True? (3D animated, 1:30)

`moms-dream-come-true-3d.mp4` is a 1280×720, 30fps, 90-second 3D animated story with a piano soundtrack.

| Time | Chapter |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Prologue (2006): Mom cooks over a fire outside a bahay kubo |
| 0:10 | Her Sacrifice (2008): her small roadside carinderia |
| 0:22 | Your Promise (2018): graduation day |
| 0:34 | The Build (2023): the restaurant rises brick by brick |
| 0:48 | Opening Day (2024): "MAMA'S KITCHEN" opens |
| 1:02 | Home (2025): Mom walks into her dream house |
| 1:16 | Salamat, Ma (2026): fireworks and sky lanterns |

`source/` holds the Three.js scene (`mom.js`), the frame renderer (`render.js`) and the soundtrack generator (`audio.py`). Sign text, names and years are plain text in `mom.js`, so they are easy to personalize.
